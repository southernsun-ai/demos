# Billing cycles and invoicing

Fernpost workspaces are billed either monthly or annually. Your billing cycle is set when the workspace is created and can be changed from **Settings → Billing**.

Monthly workspaces are invoiced on the same calendar day each month, based on the day the workspace was created. Annual workspaces are invoiced once per year on the anniversary of that date, covering the full twelve-month term in advance.

Invoices are generated at 00:00 UTC on the billing date and emailed to every user with the Billing Admin role. Payment is collected automatically from the default payment method within one business day of the invoice date.

We accept major credit and debit cards, and ACH transfer for annual workspaces on the Firm plan. Invoices, receipts, and payment history are available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices** and can be downloaded as PDF.

If a payment fails, we retry twice over the following seven days and email the Billing Admin after each attempt.
