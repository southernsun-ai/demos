# Adding seats mid-cycle

Fernpost prorates seat changes made mid-cycle. When your seat count changes partway through a billing period, the amount is adjusted for the portion of the term remaining rather than billed at the full period rate.

Seats added mid-cycle are charged from the date the seat is assigned through the end of the current term. The prorated amount is your plan's per-seat rate multiplied by the number of days remaining in the term, divided by the total number of days in the term.

For example, on an annual Firm plan at $288 per seat per year, adding two seats with 90 days left in the term is charged as:

    2 × $288 × (90 / 365) = $142.03

Monthly plans use the same calculation against the days remaining in the current month.

Prorated amounts appear as a separate line item on your next invoice, labeled **Mid-cycle seat adjustment**. Seats are available to assign immediately; you do not need to wait for the invoice to use them.
