# Cancellations and refunds

You can cancel a Fernpost workspace at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel workspace**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the term you have already paid for.

After cancelling, your workspace stays fully active until the end of that term. Monthly workspaces remain active through the end of the current month; annual workspaces remain active through the end of the current twelve-month term. No further invoices are generated once a cancellation is scheduled.

New workspaces are eligible for a full refund within 14 days of the first invoice. Contact support from the Billing Admin account to request one.

After the first 14 days, we do not issue partial refunds for time remaining on a cancelled term. Your workspace stays available for the full period you paid for.

Once a term ends, workspace data is retained in read-only form for 30 days, after which it is permanently deleted. Export your data from **Settings → Data export** before that window closes.
