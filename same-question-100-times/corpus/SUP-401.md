# Support hours and escalation

Fernpost support is staffed Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 UTC, excluding public holidays. Requests sent outside those hours are queued and picked up the next working day.

Our response targets are one working day for Solo and Studio workspaces, and four working hours for Firm workspaces. Response targets cover the first reply, not resolution.

Reach support from the in-app help menu or by email. Include your workspace name and, where relevant, the invoice number.

**Billing escalation.** Questions about charges, invoice amounts, proration, plan changes, and account credits are handled by our billing team rather than front-line support. If your question needs a decision about an amount on your account, we pass it to billing, who reply directly. Billing operates on the same hours and works to the same response targets.

Front-line support cannot approve adjustments to an invoice. Anything affecting what you are charged is confirmed by the billing team first.
