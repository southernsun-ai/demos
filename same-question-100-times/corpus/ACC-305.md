# Roles and seat assignment

Seats are managed from **Account → Roles**. Every person who signs in to the workspace occupies one seat for as long as they hold one.

**Assigning a seat.** Select **Invite user**, enter an email address, and choose a role. The invitation occupies a seat as soon as it is sent, not when it is accepted. If you have no seats free, the invite is blocked until one is available.

**Reassigning a seat.** To move a seat between people, release it from the current holder and invite the new user. The seat stays with your workspace throughout.

**Releasing a seat.** Open the user's row and select **Release seat**. The user immediately loses access to the workspace, and their records — invoices, clients, and comments — stay in place and are reattributed to the workspace owner. The seat returns to your available pool and can be reassigned right away.

Only Workspace Admins and Billing Admins can assign or release seats.
