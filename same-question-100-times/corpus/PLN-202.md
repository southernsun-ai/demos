# Plan tiers and seat limits

Fernpost is sold per seat on three plans.

**Solo** — $12 per seat per month, or $144 per seat per year. One seat only. Includes invoicing, recurring invoices, and payment collection.

**Studio** — $18 per seat per month, or $216 per seat per year. Up to 10 seats. Adds client records, estimates, expense tracking, and two user roles.

**Firm** — $24 per seat per month, or $288 per seat per year. Up to 50 seats. Adds custom roles, approval routing, multi-entity accounting, and the reporting API.

Annual billing locks your per-seat rate for the full term. Rates are not discounted for annual terms; the benefit is a fixed rate and a single invoice.

Every user who signs in to the workspace occupies one seat. You cannot exceed your plan's seat ceiling — to go past it, change plans from **Settings → Billing → Plan**. Plan changes take effect immediately.
